# A Poem for Beats

In servers far and systems wide,
Where data flows like digital tide,
The Beats keep watch with silent grace,
Light footprints in the cyber space.

Filebeat trails the logs that tell
Of applications running well,
While Metricbeat with steady hand
Measures the pulse across the land.

Heartbeat pings with caring touch,
"Are you there?" it asks so much,
And Packetbeat with eagle eye
Watches traffic flowing by.

Auditbeat guards the sacred trust,
Of files and changes, line by dust,
While Winlogbeat on Windows waits
To capture what the system states.

Built on libbeat's sturdy frame,
Each shipper earns its rightful name,
Lightweight, swift, and ever true,
They carry data, me and you.

From Go's concurrency they're born,
To Elasticsearch they're drawn,
Through Logstash or direct they send
The stories on which we depend.

So here's to Beats, both proud and small,
Who listen to our systems' call,
And ship with care from here to there
The operational data that we share.

---

*This poem celebrates the Beats project's mission of lightweight, efficient data shipping. Each stanza honors the purpose of different Beats while capturing the essence of their simplicity and reliability in the observability ecosystem.*
